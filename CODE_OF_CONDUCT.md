# TuStudyHub Code of Conduct

We aim to maintain a respectful and productive learning environment.

## Expected
- Treat teammates, classmates, lecturers and reviewers respectfully.
- Discuss disagreements constructively and give credit for work.
- Identify your own contributions honestly and be able to explain them.
- Protect private information and respect copyright.
- Communicate blockers early and propose a recovery plan.
- Use AI tools responsibly and disclose assistance.

## Unacceptable
Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sabotage, plagiarism, sharing confidential information, or knowingly submitting work you cannot explain.

## Reporting
Raise concerns privately with the project lead or lecturer. For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
